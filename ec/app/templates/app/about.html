About Us


Bikenigga is a public limited company with its registered and corporate office in Nairobi, Kenya. The company was incorporated in 2018, and our eCommerce operations officially launched in 2020. We are committed to offering high-quality bicycles and cycling accessories, focusing on durability, affordability, and customer satisfaction. Today, Bicycles is one of the fastest-growing public listed companies in Kenya’s online retail and mobility solutions space.

Our Mission


At Bikenigga, our mission is to make high-quality, affordable bicycles accessible to every Kenyan. We aim to empower mobility, promote eco-friendly transportation, and support a healthier lifestyle through our user-friendly online platform. By partnering with trusted local and international brands, and embracing innovation, we strive to deliver a seamless shopping experience while driving positive change in the cycling community.
